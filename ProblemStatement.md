# Problem Statement

Managing personal or business expenses manually using paper-based logs or even complex spreadsheets can be error-prone and time-consuming. Common issues include:

- **Difficulty in Categorization**: Organizing expenses into categories (e.g., Food, Transport, Bills) manually is tedious.
- **Error-Prone Totaling**: Manually calculating totals increases the risk of mathematical errors.
- **Search Inefficiency**: Finding a specific past expense in a physical notebook or a large spreadsheet can be difficult.
- **Data Persistence**: Paper logs can be lost or damaged, and spreadsheets can be accidentally overwritten without a clear history.

The lack of a dedicated, simple tool for expense management often leads to poor financial awareness and difficulty in budgeting.
